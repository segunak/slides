Scenario 1: AI Enhanced Group Projects
Ellen’s team uses AI for their big group project, like a digital wizard that does half the work. They get a good grade but wonder if they relied too much on their AI wizard.
​
​
Is using Generative AI this way cool or not cool?
Cool!
As long as we’re being honest and accountable.
Having an AI wizard is cool for easing work, but we must be clear about how much is our effort versus AI's magic. It's crucial to give proper credit and take responsibility for the project's outcome. So, while AI can be a big help, honesty about its use and taking accountability for the work keeps things fair and genuine.
Discussion Questions
1.How would you decide the fair share of credit between your team's work and the AI's contribution?
2.If the AI makes a mistake, who should be held accountable, and how would you address it?
3.How can teams ensure transparency when presenting AI-assisted projects?
Scenario 2: AI Homework
Josh stumbles upon a cool new app that promises to do all your homework for you. Just type in the assignment, and boom, it spits out a complete essay in minutes. With a huge English essay due tomorrow and zero words on the page, he thinks, "Why not?" and hands in the AI's work as his own, crossing fingers for an easy A.
​
​
Is using Generative AI this way cool or not cool?
Not Cool!
It’s important to make sure you’re truly learning!
It's like using a cheat code for a video game but for real life. This totally skips the point of learning and growing from doing your own work. It's unfair to everyone else and, honestly, it's like lying about who did the work. Plus, if you get caught, you're in big trouble.
Discussion Questions
1.How would you feel if you went to a doctor, engineer, or another professional, and found out they didn't really know what they were doing because they used AI to get through school?
2.What impact could that have on you or society?
3.What would you do if your friend got a scholarship for an essay that you knew was written by AI?
Scenario 3: AI-Generated Art and Music
Deetya uses an AI app that generates cool art and music from text. She starts getting tons of likes online. People start arguing whether it's really "art" if a computer helps make it.
​
​
Is using Generative AI this way cool or not cool?
Controversial
This is a tricky one, let’s talk about it!
The use of AI to generate art and music raises questions about the authenticity and value of creative expression. It's like using a fancy camera filter that makes your photos look pro. Sure, the results are awesome, but it sparks a debate: is the beauty in the filter or the person who clicked the button?
Discussion Questions
1.If a computer program draws a picture, can we hang it in a museum next to paintings by famous artists?
2.If you found out your favorite online influencer was a computer program, how would you feel?
3.Imagine if your favorite song was made by a computer. Would you feel different about it?
4.Should there be a rule that says influencers and artists must tell you if they’re AI?
​
Scenario 4: AI Personalized Learning
Blake is struggling with understanding math concepts. He decides to use an AI-powered tutoring app to get personalized help and practice problems.
​
​
Is using Generative AI this way cool or not cool?
Cool!
With a few words of caution.
AI tutoring apps are great for getting extra help, especially for tough subjects like math. But, relying too much on them could make us less able to solve problems on our own. Plus, not everyone might have access to these apps.
Discussion Questions
1.Is using AI for homework like using a calculator for every math problem, even the easy ones?
2.How do we avoid becoming lazy thinkers?
3.What if only some students can get these apps because they're expensive?
