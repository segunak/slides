Now We’re Ready For The Fancy AI Terms!
Machine Learning
The overall field of teaching computers to learn from examples, rather than following fixed instructions like in traditional computer programming.
Neural Networks
A type of Machine Learning made up of lots of tiny programs (like digital neurons) working together in layers.
Each one looks at a small part of the data (like words or pixels). As they share what they find, the network slowly learns patterns, one layer at a time.
Deep Learning
A neural network with many more layers of digital neurons, small programs that each handle a piece of the data (like words, images, or sounds).
More layers = more chances to spot complex patterns and make better predictions.
Large Language Models are…
A type of Generative AI that specializes in language.
It learns by reading massive amounts of text and predicts what words come next to generate human-like responses
GPT stands for…
Generative: Creates new text by predicting what comes next, using patterns it learned from tons of reading.
Pre-trained: Was trained ahead of time on books, websites, social media, and more to understand language and context.
Transformer: A model that focuses on the most important words in a sentence, like highlighting key info to better understand meaning.

It’s Complicatedly Simple
•AI is Patterns, Not Magic: It finds patterns and predicts what comes next using math, not meaning.
•It Learns By Guessing: Whether from labeled data (supervised) or by filling in blanks (self-supervised), it improves by trial and error.
•Big Models Need Big Math: Billions of guesses, massive datasets, and powerful computers make AI feel “smart.”
•It Doesn’t Truly Understand: AI doesn’t “know”. It recognizes patterns, not meaning (until we reach AGI, artificial general intelligence).
•Humans Still Matter: We guide it. Our feedback shapes what it learns and how it behaves
AI learns from patterns, not meaning. Humans provide the goals. Math does the work.
