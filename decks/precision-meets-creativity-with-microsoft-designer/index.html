Writing Matters: The Power of a Good Prompt
·Specific words create specific results: AI follows the details you provide—small changes in wording lead to big differences.
·Clearer prompts = better outputs: The more precise you are, the more AI can generate exactly what you want.
·AI isn’t guessing—it’s following your lead: The way you write a prompt shapes what AI creates, just like giving clear instructions to a person.
·English class just got real: Writing clearly and with detail isn’t just for essays—it’s the key to getting AI to work for you.

Prompt Engineering is…
The process of writing detailed instructions for AI to get the best and most accurate results.
AI only responds based on what you tell it, so the better your prompt, the better your result. Knowing how to craft clear, detailed, and creative prompts helps you get AI to generate useful, high-quality content.
​
Key Takeaways
·AI follows your instructions. The better your prompt, the better your results.
·Details matter. Specific words shape how AI interprets your ideas.
·Creativity + Clarity = Great AI content. The best prompts mix both!
·Prompt engineering is an important skill. Knowing how to guide AI is valuable for the future!
